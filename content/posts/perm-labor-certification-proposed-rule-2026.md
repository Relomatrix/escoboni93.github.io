On November 17, 2026, the US Department of Labor proposed the biggest rewrite of PERM labor certification since 2004. Employers sponsoring EB-2 and EB-3 green cards would recruit online for at least 90 days, face a two-year layoff bar and lose Schedule A for nurses. It is only a proposal: comments close January 19, 2027.

For related US changes, see our articles on [the proposed 60-day H-1B grace period](/blog/h1b-60-day-grace-period-proposed-rule-2026/) and on [the end of automatic EAD extensions](/blog/ead-automatic-extension-ended-final-rule-2026/).

## TL;DR

* **What**: DOL/ETA proposed rule, *Modernizing the Labor Market Test and Improving Protections for U.S. Workers in the PERM Immigrant Visa Program*, 91 FR 73916, RIN 1205-AC29.
* **Status**: proposal only. **Comments until January 19, 2027**, docket **ETA-2026-0133**. Today's PERM rules still apply.
* **Recruitment**: print newspaper ads replaced by online and in-person channels; at least **90 calendar days**; recruitment finished **30 to 60 days** before filing.
* **Layoffs**: no filing if the employer laid off US workers in the same or similar occupation and area within **2 years**.
* **Schedule A**: **eliminated**, so registered nurses and physical therapists would go through full PERM.
* **Filing**: recruitment documents, including US applicants' résumés, submitted **with** the application, not only on audit.

## Why PERM matters

PERM labor certification is the first step for most employer-sponsored green cards in the EB-2 and EB-3 categories. Before filing Form ETA-9089, the employer tests the US labor market and shows that no able, willing, qualified and available US worker applied. The [Federal Register notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/17/2026-23370/modernizing-the-labor-market-test-and-improving-protections-for-us-workers-in-the-perm-immigrant) says the rules have not been updated in nearly 20 years, while remote work and electronic recruiting have changed how jobs are filled.

## Recruitment: online, longer, closer to filing

Under the current rule, recruitment generally takes place 30 to 180 days before filing: a 30-day job order with the State Workforce Agency (SWA) and print advertisements on two different Sundays, plus extra steps for professional jobs.

The proposal would:

* replace the elective menu with **electronic recruitment** on the employer's website, job search websites, social media platforms, and job fairs or on-campus recruiting;
* keep ads, including the SWA job order, open for **at least 90 calendar days**, or longer if the employer usually advertises comparable non-PERM jobs for longer;
* require all recruitment to end **no more than 60 and no fewer than 30 calendar days** before filing;
* require the **offered wage** or wage range in the application and ads, at or above the prevailing wage.

## Two-year layoff bar

Today an employer with recent layoffs must notify and consider laid-off US workers. Under the proposal, the employer would have to attest that it has **not laid off US workers** in the **same or similar occupation** in the area of intended employment within **2 years** before filing. If layoffs happen after filing, the duty to consider qualified laid-off US workers would continue until the I-140 petition is approved (or the application or petition is denied or withdrawn), and the employer would have to document that it offered them the job. DOL asks whether 2 years is the right period.

## End of Schedule A

Schedule A lets employers skip the labor market test for pre-certified occupations. According to DOL, registered nurses and physical therapists have been the only listed occupations since the 1990s; a separate group covers certain people of exceptional ability in the sciences or arts. DOL proposes to **stop administering Schedule A**, so these jobs would need a full PERM application.

## What it means for workers abroad and employers

| Situation | What to watch |
| --- | --- |
| PERM already filed | Current rules apply; the proposal is not final |
| Planning to start PERM in 2027 | Budget for a 90-day recruitment window plus 30 to 60 days before filing |
| Employer with recent layoffs | Check the proposed 2-year bar for the same or similar occupation |
| Foreign nurse or physical therapist | Schedule A shortcut would disappear if adopted |
| Fully remote role | DOL seeks better ways to define the area of employment for remote and telework jobs |

## How to comment

Comments go through Regulations.gov under docket **ETA-2026-0133** by **January 19, 2027**, as stated in the Federal Register notice. DOL must consider comments before publishing a final rule, which may differ from this proposal.
