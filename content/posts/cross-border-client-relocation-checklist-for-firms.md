When a client relocates between two countries, a firm should check at least five things before advising: where tax residence stays, registration duties such as Italy's AIRE, which social security system applies, which official sources were used and in which version, and whether there is a conflict of interest. Here is how to set them up in order.

I wrote this for law and tax firms handling clients between Italy, the UK, Switzerland, France, the US, Argentina and Brazil. It does not replace professional advice: it lines up the questions that, if skipped at intake, come back later in the matter.

## TL;DR

* **Tax residence**: since 2024 Italian registry enrolment is a rebuttable presumption; domicile and physical presence for most of the year weigh in.
* **AIRE**: Italians who move their habitual residence abroad register with the consulate within 90 days, but this alone does not settle tax residence.
* **Social security**: within the EU one legislation applies as a rule (Reg. 883/2004); outside the EU, bilateral agreements.
* **Sources**: record which version of a law, form or guidance note you relied on.
* **Conflicts and quote**: check them before accepting the engagement, not after.

## 1. Where tax residence stays

This comes first because almost everything else depends on it. Following Legislative Decree 209 of 27 December 2023 and the Italian Revenue Agency's [Circular 20/E of 4 November 2024](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/cs-4-novembre-2024), a person is Italian tax resident if, for most of the tax year, any one of these applies:

* civil-law residence;
* domicile, meaning the place of personal and family ties;
* physical presence (183 days, 184 in leap years, including part days);
* registration in the population register, now only a rebuttable presumption.

In practice: at the first meeting, collect departure and return dates, where the family lives and where the main home is. If both countries treat the client as resident, the double tax treaty between them, where there is one, decides.

## 2. Registration duties: AIRE and the destination country

An Italian citizen who moves their habitual residence abroad must [register with AIRE](https://www.esteri.it/it/servizi-opportunita/italiani-all-estero/aire_0/) at the competent consulate within 90 days, usually through the Fast It portal. People staying abroad for less than 12 months do not register.

Two common mistakes: treating AIRE as proof of non-residence for tax (it is not, see point 1), and forgetting the destination country's own registration deadlines.

## 3. Which social security applies

Within the European Union, [Regulation (EC) No 883/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/IT/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32004R0883) makes a person subject to one State's legislation only, usually the State where they work, with exceptions such as posting. Outside the EU, bilateral agreements apply: for the United States, the Social Security Administration describes the [agreement with Italy](https://www.ssa.gov/international/Agreement_Pamphlets/italy.html), and we covered it in [US-Italy totalization and remote work](/blog/us-italy-totalization-agreement-remote-work-2026/).

Ask early: is the client employed by an Italian employer, a foreign one, or self-employed? The answer changes which certificate to request and from which agency.

## 4. Which sources, in which version

Laws, forms and official guidance change often, sometimes in the middle of a matter. For every key conclusion, record the official source, the date you consulted it and its version. When a source changes, it is then clear which opinions need another look.

## 5. Conflicts of interest, documents and the quote

Before accepting the engagement: check the firm does not already act for parties with opposing interests (common in family, inheritance or employer-employee matters), ask for the full list of documents up front, and send a clear quote. These steps are simple, and skipping them is what costs the most time later.

## FAQ

### Does AIRE registration rule out Italian tax residence?

No. Since 2024 registration in the Italian population register is only a rebuttable presumption; civil residence, domicile (personal and family ties) and physical presence for most of the tax year also count, as the Italian Revenue Agency explains in Circular 20/E of 2024.

### When must an Italian citizen register with AIRE?

According to the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the declaration goes to the competent consular office within 90 days of moving residence abroad.

### Which social security applies to someone working in another EU country?

Regulation (EC) No 883/2004 coordinates social security systems: as a rule only one State's legislation applies, usually where the work is done, with exceptions such as posting. Outside the EU, bilateral agreements apply where they exist.

### Why record the version of each source in the file?

Because laws, forms and official guidance change. Knowing which version supported an opinion tells the firm whether to revisit it when the source is updated.
