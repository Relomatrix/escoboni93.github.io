If you plan to file an EB-5 investor petition, the USCIS fee depends on the postmark: from November 30, 2026, Form I-526 costs $7,615 and an initial Form I-526E costs $7,850 plus a $1,100 Integrity Fund fee. Filings postmarked by November 29 keep the old fees. The investment minimums do not change with this rule.

For the wider picture of moving and working across borders, see our pillar on [cross-border employment](/blog/cross-border-employment/) and our piece on the [USCIS deference rule for extensions](/blog/uscis-deference-rule-h1b-extensions-2026/).

## TL;DR

* **Rule**: DHS final rule "USCIS EB-5 Fee Rule", [FR Doc. 2026-20016](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/30/2026-20016/us-citizenship-and-immigration-services-employment-based-immigrant-visa-fifth-preference-eb-5-fee), published **September 30, 2026**.
* **Effective**: **November 30, 2026**, based on the **postmark** date.
* **Investors**: I-526 **$7,615**; initial I-526E **$7,850** + **$1,100** Integrity Fund fee; I-829 **$5,000**.
* **Average increase**: DHS estimates EB-5 form fees rise by about **70.7%** on a weighted average.
* **Not changed**: the investment amounts. USCIS says they **adjust automatically on January 1, 2027**.

## What the final rule does

USCIS [announced on September 29, 2026](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/uscis-adjusts-eb-5-fees-to-cover-program-costs-and-strengthen-integrity-measures) that it is updating EB-5 fees so that they recover the full cost of the program, meet statutory processing time goals and fund integrity measures such as audits and site visits. The EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022 required a program-specific fee study.

The final rule also codifies parts of the 2022 Act and responds to comments on the proposed rule of October 23, 2025. DHS explains that, after a November 12, 2025 decision of the US District Court for the District of Colorado (*Moody v. Noem*), USCIS had reverted to the EB-5 fees in effect until March 31, 2024. Those are the "current" fees the new schedule replaces.

## The new fees

| Form | Who files it | Fee from Nov 30, 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-526 | Standalone investor | $7,615 (includes $75 technology fee) |
| I-526E, initial | Regional center investor | $7,850 (includes $75 technology fee) |
| I-526E, amendment | Regional center investor | $7,775 |
| EB-5 Integrity Fund petition fee | With each I-526E | $1,100 (was $1,000) |
| I-829 | Removal of conditions | $5,000 |
| I-527 (new) | Legacy I-526 amendment after a regional center termination or debarment | $10,330 |
| I-956, initial / amendment | Regional center designation | $44,115 / $9,835 |
| I-956F | Investment in a commercial enterprise | $42,675 |
| I-956G | Regional center annual statement | $2,165 |
| Regional center annual Integrity Fund fee | Regional centers, due October 1 | $22,000, or $11,000 with 20 or fewer investors |

Source: final rule text amending 8 CFR 106.2. DHS says all adjusted fees are incorporated in the revised Form G-1055, Fee Schedule.

## Investment amounts: a separate change on January 1, 2027

The fee rule does not touch the investment thresholds. According to the [USCIS Policy Manual](https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-6-part-g-chapter-1), petitions filed on or after March 15, 2022 require **$1,050,000**, or **$800,000** for a targeted employment area or an infrastructure project. USCIS states that these amounts **automatically adjust on January 1, 2027**, and every 5 years after that, under INA 203(b)(5)(C)(iii). As of today no official adjusted figure has been published in the sources we checked.

The Policy Manual also notes that EB-5 visas for investors in the Regional Center Program are authorized through **September 30, 2027**.

## What to check before filing

* **Postmark, not signing date**: the rule applies the new fees to anything postmarked on or after November 30, 2026.
* **Exact amount**: check the current Form G-1055, Fee Schedule, on uscis.gov on the day you file.
* **Two payments for I-526E**: the petition fee and the Integrity Fund fee are separate amounts.
* **Budget for 2027**: if you file after January 1, 2027, the investment minimum may also differ.
