From 30 November 2026, international students applying for a UK Student visa must show £1,570 a month for courses in London and £1,203 a month elsewhere, up to nine months, on top of unpaid course fees. Applications made before that date are still assessed under the old amounts of £1,529 and £1,171.

## TL;DR

* **Legal basis**: Statement of changes to the Immigration Rules **HC 584**, laid before Parliament on 3 September 2026 under [section 3(2) of the Immigration Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/77/section/3), paragraphs APP ST8 to ST10.
* **Start date**: **30 November 2026**; applications made before that date follow the rules in force on 29 November 2026.
* **London**: **£1,570** per month (was £1,529), maximum **£14,130** for 9 months.
* **Outside London**: **£1,203** per month (was £1,171), maximum **£10,827** for 9 months.
* **Accommodation deposit offset**: cap rises to **£1,570**.

## The new monthly amounts

[HC 584](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026-accessible) replaces the figures in paragraph ST 12.3 of [Appendix Student](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student):

| Place of study | Before 30 Nov 2026 | From 30 Nov 2026 | Maximum (9 months) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| London | £1,529 per month | **£1,570** per month | **£14,130** |
| Outside London | £1,171 per month | **£1,203** per month | **£10,827** |

The monthly amount is required for each month of the course, up to nine months, in addition to any outstanding course fees shown on the Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS). If the course includes part of a month, the period is rounded up to the next full month. Students at a residential independent school must instead show enough to pay outstanding course and boarding fees for one academic year.

Example: for a nine-month master's course in Manchester with fees already paid in full, an applicant on or after 30 November 2026 must show £10,827 instead of £10,539.

## Who is affected and who is exempt

Under Appendix Student, the funds requirement in ST 12.3 applies to:

* applicants for **entry clearance** (applying from outside the UK);
* applicants for **permission to stay** who have been in the UK with permission for **less than 12 months**.

You meet the financial requirement **without showing funds** if you apply for permission to stay after living in the UK with permission for 12 months or more, or if you apply as a Student Union Sabbatical Officer.

Other rules stay the same: funds must normally be held for a **28-day period** as set out in Appendix Finance (unless you rely on a student loan or official financial sponsorship), and course fees already paid to the sponsor must be confirmed on the CAS or by a sponsor receipt.

## Accommodation deposit offset

If you have paid a deposit to your sponsor for accommodation the sponsor arranged, you can subtract it from the funds required. HC 584 raises the maximum offset from £1,529 to **£1,570** from 30 November 2026.

## Why the amounts change

The [explanatory memorandum to HC 584](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026/explanatory-memorandum-to-the-statement-of-changes-in-immigration-rules-hc-584-3-september-2026-accessible) explains that the maintenance requirement is aligned with the maintenance loans available to home students, which the Department for Education reviews each year. The new figures match the 2026/2027 academic year, and the Home Office says it will keep them aligned in future.

## Erasmus+ changes already in force

The same statement prepares for the UK's association to **Erasmus+ in 2027**, agreed by the UK Government and the European Commission on 17 December 2025, according to the explanatory memorandum. Changes that took effect on 8 October 2026 allow Erasmus+ participants at a participating sponsor to:

* be sponsored on the Student route even if the course does not lead to an approved qualification;
* study at RQF level 2 or above (SCQF level 5 in Scotland);
* take work placements without the usual duration limits.

The memorandum also explains that Erasmus+ participants can use the Visitor route for some short activities, including study placements of up to six months at state-funded schools and academies.

## FAQ

**How much money do I need from 30 November 2026?**
Outstanding course fees plus £1,570 a month in London or £1,203 a month elsewhere, up to nine months.

**I apply before 30 November 2026. Which amounts apply?**
The previous ones (£1,529 and £1,171), because applications made before 30 November are decided under the rules in force on 29 November 2026.

**Who is exempt?**
Applicants for permission to stay who have lived in the UK with permission for at least 12 months, and Student Union Sabbatical Officers.

**Can my accommodation deposit count?**
Yes, a deposit paid to your sponsor for sponsor-arranged accommodation can be offset, up to £1,570 from 30 November 2026.
