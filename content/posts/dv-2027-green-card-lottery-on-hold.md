The DV-2027 green card lottery is still on hold. The State Department has not opened registration and has given no start date. On 29 October 2026 the Ninth Circuit stayed a court order that would have forced registration to open by 1 November, so the old November and December dates are no longer in force.

If you are weighing other US routes, see our guides on the [proposed H-1B 60-day grace period rule](/blog/h1b-60-day-grace-period-proposed-rule-2026/) and the [end of automatic EAD extensions](/blog/ead-automatic-extension-ended-final-rule-2026/).

## TL;DR

* **Status**: DV-2027 registration is **not open** and the State Department has announced **no start date**.
* **Court**: on **29 October 2026** the Ninth Circuit stayed the order requiring registration by 1 November 2026 (Red Eagle Law v. Rubio, No. 26-6943).
* **Application period**: unchanged at **1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027** for anyone eventually selected.
* **Program size**: up to **55,000** visas a year under the INA.
* **Scams**: no one can take or guarantee a DV-2027 entry today.

## What the State Department says

On its [DV-2027 entry period page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/changes-to-2027-dv-program-entry-period.html), the State Department says it is implementing certain changes to the Diversity Visa entry process. It will announce the start date of the DV-2027 registration period "as soon as practicable", together with the date selection results may be available through Entry Status Check. The visa application period for people selected for DV-2027 stays 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027.

## The court case, in plain terms

The Ninth Circuit order, published on [govinfo.gov](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ca9-26-6943/pdf/USCOURTS-ca9-26-6943-0.pdf), sets out the timeline:

| Date | What happened (per the Ninth Circuit order) |
| --- | --- |
| August 2026 | Red Eagle Law, L.C., an immigration law firm, sued, arguing the suspension of DV-2027 was unlawful under the APA |
| 14 October 2026 | The district court ordered the Department to announce registration by 29 October, open it by 1 November and notify selectees by 3 December 2026 |
| 29 October 2026 | The Ninth Circuit granted the government's emergency motion and stayed that order pending appeal |

The panel found the government likely to succeed because the law firm had not shown it was within the "zone of interests" of the Diversity Visa statute. The court added that it does "not endorse the Department's failure to open the lottery registration period". In practice the case has not decided whether the suspension itself is lawful, and the appeal continues.

## What it means for you

* **Do not plan around DV-2027.** Until the State Department announces a registration period, there is no lottery to enter.
* **Keep watching official channels.** Dates, if any, will be published on travel.state.gov.
* **Think about parallel routes.** Family, employment or investment categories have their own rules, timelines and fees; check them on [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov/green-card).
* **Protect yourself from fraud.** No website, agent or app can register you, speed up selection or guarantee a DV visa now.

No. The State Department has not announced a start date.

**What is the DV-2027 visa application period?**
1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027, for anyone eventually selected.

**How many visas does the program award?**
Up to 55,000 a year under the INA.
