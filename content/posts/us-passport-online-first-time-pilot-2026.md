Yes, but only for some applicants. From November 16, 2026 the US State Department pilots online Form DS-11 applications on America.gov for adults in the United States who are naturalized citizens applying for the first time, or who held a valid passport but cannot renew online. Americans abroad are not included yet.

If you are planning a move, also read our guides on [FBAR rules for Americans abroad](/blog/fbar-americans-abroad-who-must-file-2026/), the [Foreign Earned Income Exclusion](/blog/foreign-earned-income-exclusion-2027/) and [ETIAS and EES for travel to Europe](/blog/etias-ees-2026-visa-free-travel-europe/).

## TL;DR

* **What**: a pilot for the online **Form DS-11**, the application normally filed in person.
* **When**: effective **November 16, 2026** to **November 20, 2027**, unless extended, rolled out in phases.
* **Who first**: adults **located in the US** who use a **USCIS Certificate of Naturalization**, or who had a fully valid passport but are **not eligible for DS-82** online renewal.
* **How**: eligibility check on **America.gov**, identity check on **Login.gov**, photo upload and a **recorded oath**.
* **Fees**: the **$35 execution fee** still applies.

## What the State Department announced

The [Federal Register notice 2026-23317](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-23317) (Public Notice 13173, scheduled for publication on November 16, 2026) explains that today only people eligible for a renewal on Form DS-82 can apply online. The pilot extends online filing to Form DS-11, used by first-time applicants and by former passport holders who cannot renew.

The State Department says it processed 13,913,231 DS-11 applications in fiscal year 2025, and that a paper application takes on average 85 minutes to complete.

## Who can use the online DS-11 pilot

| Group | Status in the pilot |
| --- | --- |
| Adults (18+) in the US with a previous fully valid passport who cannot use DS-82 | Included from the start |
| First-time adult applicants in the US with a USCIS Certificate of Naturalization | Included from the start |
| First-time applicants with an eligible US birth certificate from a participating state | Planned expansion |
| Applicants with a Certificate of Citizenship or a Consular Report of Birth Abroad (CRBA) | Planned expansion |
| Eligible applicants located overseas | Planned expansion |
| Minors under 18 | Not included |

Applicants also need an identity document accepted by Login.gov, excluding mobile driver's licenses: a valid US passport book or card, or a valid driver's license or state ID issued by a US state, the District of Columbia or a US territory. People who need to submit an affidavit or a court order are sent to the paper process.

## How the online application works

1. **Check eligibility on America.gov**: questions on whether you apply for yourself, are a US citizen and have urgent travel plans.
2. **Verify your identity on Login.gov**: ID upload, a liveness check, your Social Security Number and your address.
3. **Complete the form**: confirm the imported data, add your USCIS or alien registration number if you have one, an emergency contact and your occupation.
4. **Upload a passport photo**, choose the product and delivery, and **record yourself stating the oath**.
5. **Review by a passport agent**, then adjudication as for a paper DS-11; the passport is mailed to the address you give.

The notice states that eligibility for a US passport does not change: you must still prove citizenship and identity.

## What it means if you are moving abroad

For now the pilot helps people who are still in the United States. A naturalized citizen preparing a move to Italy or France, for example, can apply for a first passport online before leaving, instead of booking an in-person appointment. Once you are abroad you cannot use the pilot until the State Department extends it to applicants located overseas, so plan the application before you leave.

Parents of children born abroad should note that applicants using a CRBA are only in the planned expansion, and minors are not part of the pilot.

## FAQ

**Can I apply for my first US passport online?**
Only in the pilot group: adults in the US using a Certificate of Naturalization, or former passport holders who cannot use DS-82.

**How long does the pilot last?**
From November 16, 2026 to November 20, 2027, unless extended.

**Can Americans living abroad use it?**
Not yet; overseas applicants are a planned expansion.

**Do I still pay the $35 execution fee?**
Yes, it still applies during the pilot.

**How is the oath taken?**
You record yourself stating it, and a passport agent reviews the recording.

*General information, not legal advice. Check the current eligibility rules on America.gov and the State Department before applying.*
